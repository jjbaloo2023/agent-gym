# Longer ENTH calculation: protocol and feasibility

The portable restart preparation is incomplete and has not been launched or uploaded. Its first preflight stopped because the checkpoint parser expected an atom-count label that GROMACS did not emit; no extended TPR or archive was produced. Local execution is on hold while Colab feasibility is assessed. The proposed continuation uses the validated checkpoint from the Astra-assisted `validation-007` proxy; it does not create the paper's adsorbed or solvent states.

## Scientific target and limits

[Joseph et al. methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s42003-020-01471-6#Sec20) use 70 ns equilibration plus 150 ns production per configuration and analyze the final 100 ns. Their states are generated by sequential protein-center-of-mass pulling with PIP2 restraints. Their full lipid preserves the crystallographic headgroup position and orientation. The published methods do not supply the numerical pull constants, rates or state-selection coordinates.

Our proxy has 113 lipids, rather than 200. Its geometric SAPI24 placement does not guarantee the crystallographic headgroup pose. PIP2 protonation, H0 mapping and the literal 0.15 mM KCl remain documented assumptions. More compute alone does not fix these differences. Exact recreation requires a paper-matched starting system, a defensible pulling protocol/state definitions and suitable sampling; arbitrary translations must not be presented as published states.

## Achievable time

The local two-thread CPU logs measured roughly 1.6–3.25 ns/day; the latest released stage measured 2.624 ns/day. At that last rate, 24 hours gives about 2.6 ns; 72 hours about 7.9 ns; one week about 18.4 ns. These are estimates subject to load, hardware, analysis overhead and interruptions. One 220-ns state would take roughly 84 days, or about 252 days for three states before replicas, at the same rate. Full scientific replication is beyond a one-day CPU test.

The first defensible long stage is equilibration/stability assessment of the current proxy, with a checkpoint at every interruption. A later comparison must first resolve or explicitly pre-register alternative state definitions and matched sampling. Helix persistence in the initially helical starting state cannot establish lipid-induced folding.

## Portable checkpoint preparation

`seed.tpr` and `seed.cpt` are exact copies of the clean 007 released-NPT input and checkpoint. The checkpoint contains 39,045 atoms and is at released step 4,200, time 8.4 ps (28.4 ps including prior warmup). SHA256 hashes and source paths are recorded. `eq70ns.tpr` changes only the TPR endpoint to 70,000 ps at the existing 2-fs timestep; it does not generate velocities or run dynamics. `effective.mdp` records the extended parameters.

[GROMACS checkpoint continuation](https://manual.gromacs.org/2023.3/user-guide/managing-simulations.html) retains full-precision coordinates, velocities and coupling state. New output parts preserve source evidence. Compatible GROMACS 2023.3 tooling was used for preflight; changing hardware/build may change numerical trajectories and requires its own recorded validation.

The underlying restart command, after an execution environment and bounds are selected, is:

```sh
gmx mdrun -s eq70ns.tpr -cpi seed.cpt -deffnm eq -noappend -cpt 5
```

This command alone does not enforce a job budget. The eventual launcher must enforce the chosen wall time, CPU/GPU/memory limits and a 2-GiB aggregate output cap, stop gracefully before its deadline, and run one simulation at a time. CPU settings for the earlier local proposal are two threads and 4 GiB; no GPU or paid compute has been launched. Checkpoint recovery must retain the original allocation deadline unless a new budget is explicitly granted.

## Checks before scientific interpretation

Fail on LINCS/constraint/fatal errors or nonfinite observables. Record temperature, pressure, box volume, membrane leaflet geometry and PIP2 location. Follow H0 helicity with the validated DSSP mode and state the unconfirmed residue mapping. Measure protein–membrane contacts and insertion depth independently of helicity. Compare time blocks before claiming equilibration; pressure fluctuations and a stable mean temperature alone are insufficient. Record full commands, versions, source/TPR/checkpoint hashes, restart events, output sizes and elapsed time.

The eventual Figure 4 comparison needs all three independently defined states, sufficient equilibration, equal analysis windows and independent-seed uncertainty. This prepared checkpoint is a starting point for the current proxy, not a completed reproduction protocol.
