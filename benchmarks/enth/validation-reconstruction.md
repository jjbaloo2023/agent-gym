# ENTH inserted-state reconstruction plan

This is a bounded local validation of one reconstructed inserted-state system. It does not reproduce Figure 4 or score biological agreement. See [the actual validation result](validation-results.md). `scripts/enth_validation.py` uses public inputs (reusing the hashed validation-006 downloads for the Astra retry) and writes results as `validation.json` and `validation.md`. A hard timeout can interrupt those reports; supervisor manifests and partial files are retained. The worker persists a command ledger and rejects constraint errors or prematurely stopped warmup stages before further dynamics.

The source inputs are [rat epsin ENTH PDB 1H0A](https://files.rcsb.org/download/1H0A.pdb), the [MacKerell CHARMM36 July 2020 GROMACS force field](https://mackerell.umaryland.edu/download.php?filename=CHARMM_ff_params_files/charmm36-jul2020.ff.tgz), the [CHARMM-GUI 128/256-lipid POPC bilayer archive](https://www.charmm-gui.org/archive/pure_bilayer/popc.tar.gz), and the CHARMM-GUI [POPC](https://www.charmm-gui.org/archive/csml/popc.pdb) and [SAPI24](https://www.charmm-gui.org/archive/csml/sapi24.pdb) templates. The CHARMM-GUI archive identifies SAPI24 as PI(4,5)P2 protonated on P4 with net charge −4. PDB 1H0A is rat ENTH chain A, 158 residues, with bound Ins(1,4,5)P3.

The reconstruction uses the smaller 128-lipid POPC template to stay under the 100,000-atom cap after explicit solvation. The archive's bilayer coordinates use legacy CHARMM27 atom names, so the script derives a name translation to the CHARMM36 POPC residue using a colored molecular-graph isomorphism between the archive PSF and the current CHARMM36 RTP; coordinates and force-field parameters are not altered. It translates the archived coordinates into their declared cell, places the protein by aligning the crystallographic InsP3 centroid with the upper POPC phosphate plane, and points the protein C-alpha centroid away from the bilayer. One upper-leaflet POPC nearest that anchor is replaced by SAPI24. POPCs whose heavy atoms approach the protein within 3 Å are removed. The script rejects the geometry if the protein has less than 12 Å clearance from an xy periodic image. These are explicit geometric choices because the original CHARMM-GUI job and placement coordinates were not supplied.

The paper's 0.15 mM KCl value is kept literally. At this small system volume, the requested added neutral salt pair count is expected to round to zero; GROMACS still adds counterions to neutralize the system. This does not substitute 0.15 M. The paper's exact H0 mapping is unavailable; for this structural pilot only, chain A residues 1–18 are treated as H0. The P4-protonated SAPI24 choice is documented, not confirmed as the authors' protonation.

The simulation budget is at most 50,000 steps at 2 fs: 5,000 restrained NVT steps with a 100-to-303.15 K temperature ramp, 5,000 restrained semi-isotropic NPT steps, then 40,000 unrestrained semi-isotropic NPT steps at 303.15 K and 1 bar. The Astra retry uses two steepest-descent stages, each capped at 5,000 iterations and 540 seconds within the shared 30-minute workflow limit. First, `constraints=none` allows covalent bonds involving hydrogen to relax under their force-field potentials at `emstep=0.01` nm. Rigid TIP3P water remains under SETTLE. Second, `constraints=h-bonds` and `emstep=0.001` nm verify compatibility with the MD constraint manifold. Both stages must converge below 1,000 kJ/mol/nm and report no constraint error before dynamics starts. LINCS settings and warning thresholds are not weakened. [GROMACS 2023.3 documents the distinction between converted bond constraints and rigid-water SETTLE](https://manual.gromacs.org/2023.3/user-guide/mdp-options.html#bonds). This two-stage protocol is a documented repair choice, not the authors' original protocol. All GROMACS dynamics request two CPU threads and CPU-only nonbonded, PME, and bonded calculations. The parent runner supplies the process-group CPU, memory, wall-time, and output-size boundary.

The retry's original worker retained a six-minute NVT sublimit and stopped at 7 ps. A checkpoint continuation completed the original 10-ps endpoint. A new guard then rejected the valid endpoint because its final GRO title lacked time; the guard was corrected to use the explicit final log step/time row plus final coordinates. A second continuation proceeded from completed NVT without repeating MD steps. All segments share the original start time and absolute 30-minute deadline; setup and intervention gaps consume that same budget. Original and intermediate reports and executed sources are retained. The current repository worker removes the dynamics substage time caps and uses the common supervisor deadline. The actual executed worker is identified by its saved hash. [GROMACS checkpoint continuation preserves the simulation state and checks output-file consistency for appending](https://manual.gromacs.org/2023.3/user-guide/managing-simulations.html).

After dynamics, independent analysis extracts Potential, Temperature, Pressure, and Volume; checks those values for finiteness; scans every minimization and dynamics log for LINCS/constraint warnings; and checks that both phosphate-defined leaflets remain present with a plausible separation. Explicit log step/time rows and coordinate/trajectory times determine completion, since a graceful stop also prints `Finished mdrun`. A periodic heavy-atom contact audit describes changes across ionized, relaxed and constrained-minimized structures. DSSP assignments require a starting-structure positive control. Even if all checks pass, the short run supports workflow execution and coarse physical checks only. It does not test pulling, adsorbed or solution states, H0 folding, the published helicity ranking, or biological agreement.
